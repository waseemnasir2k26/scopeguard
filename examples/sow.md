# Statement of Work: Harbor Light Studio website

Synthetic example. Client: Harbor Light Studio (contact: Dana Okafor, dana@harborlight.example). Freelancer: Alex Rivera. Fixed price: USD 3,200.

## 1. Deliverables

1.1 A five-page responsive website: Home, About, Services, Portfolio and Contact.
1.2 The Portfolio page includes an image gallery of up to 24 client-supplied images.
1.3 A contact form on the Contact page that emails each enquiry to one address supplied by the client.
1.4 Responsive layout that works on phones, tablets and desktop browsers.
1.5 Basic on-page SEO: page titles, meta descriptions and image alt text.
1.6 Installation of Google Analytics using the client's account.
1.7 Social media icon links in the footer pointing to the client's existing profiles.
1.8 Image optimisation for fast loading, and deployment to the client's hosting.
1.9 The client supplies all copy, logo files and photographs before design starts.

## 2. Revisions

2.1 The price includes two rounds of revisions on the design.
2.2 Each round is one consolidated set of feedback from the client; further rounds are billed hourly.

## 3. Exclusions

3.1 E-commerce, an online shop, shopping cart or payment processing are not included.
3.2 A blog, and the writing of blog posts or any other copywriting, are not included.
3.3 Translation or a multilingual version of the site is not included.
3.4 Logo or brand design, photography and video production are not included.
3.5 Booking or appointment systems are not included.
3.6 Newsletter or email marketing setup (for example Mailchimp) is not included.
3.7 Ongoing SEO, paid advertising, hosting, domain fees and maintenance after launch are not included.

## 4. Timeline

4.1 The project runs four weeks from kickoff.
4.2 First design drafts are delivered within one week of receiving the client's content.
4.3 The client returns feedback within three business days of each delivery.
